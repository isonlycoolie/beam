title: Principles
description: The decisions behind modern, fast, accessible UI. Draft, to be agreed.

## Type

1. **One family, two at most.** A display face plus a text face is the ceiling.
2. **A real scale.** 5 to 7 sizes from a ratio between 1.2 and 1.333.
3. **Tighter headings, looser body.** Headings at 1.0 to 1.2 line height with slight negative tracking; body at 1.5 to 1.7.
4. **Measure.** Body text runs 60 to 75 characters per line.

## Space

5. **4px grid.** Every padding, margin and gap is a multiple of 4.
6. **Group by proximity.** Space inside a group is at least 2x smaller than space between groups.

## Colour

7. **Neutrals first.** Most of the surface is neutral; one accent marks actions.
8. **Contrast.** 4.5:1 for body text against the real background.
9. **No default palette.** Framework defaults are not a brand.

## Layout and interaction

10. **Left-aligned reading.**
11. **Unequal emphasis.** The primary item leads; not every card is the same.
12. **Targets of at least 24px**, 44px on touch, with a visible focus ring.
13. **Motion with purpose.** 150 to 300ms, ease-out, and respect reduced motion.

## Performance

14. **Under 1.5 MB** on first load.
15. **Images** in AVIF or WebP, sized per breakpoint, with width and height set.
16. **At most 4 font files**, with `font-display: swap`.
