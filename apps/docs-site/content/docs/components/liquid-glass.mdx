## Tune it

Four controls shape the material. Their defaults are iOS 26's, as glisse measured them: drag the sliders, then copy the CSS onto any element (or a whole page) to tune every Liquid Glass inside it.

<LiquidGlassTuner />

| Control | What it does | iOS default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bend | How far the rim bends what's behind it. Only a band along the edge bends, 0.3 of the shorter side and at most 36px; the middle stays clear | `1` |
| Dispersion | Colour split at the edge: red bends a little further than blue | `0.6` (12%) |
| Frost | Blur behind the glass. Keep it thin: heavy blur turns glass into frosted plastic | `3px` |
| Rim | The line of light on the edge, lit from the top left with a fainter reflection bottom right | `0.3` light, `0.13` dark |

## Usage

```tsx
import { LiquidGlass } from "@/components/ui/liquid-glass";

<LiquidGlass pressable className="px-8 py-5">
  <span className="font-semibold">Now playing</span>
</LiquidGlass>;
```

Use it for one floating surface over photos, video, maps or a scrolling feed: a bar, a toolbar, a button. For whole cards, menus and dialogs, switch the page to [glass mode](/docs/components/glass) instead.

## Browser support

The bending runs in Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, Android). Safari and Firefox show the frost and rim without it. `prefers-reduced-transparency` gives a solid surface and `prefers-reduced-motion` stops the press spring.
