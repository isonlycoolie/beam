---
title: Where glass belongs
description: Glass is a material for floating layers, not a coat of paint for every card.
---

| Good | Why |
| --- | --- |
| A floating navigation bar over scrolling content | The blur shows depth and keeps context |
| Controls over media, maps or photos | Controls stay readable without hiding the content |
| Sheets, popovers and menus | Temporary layers feel above the page |
| One hero card over a rich image | A single focal surface |

| Avoid | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Glass on a flat background | Nothing to blur, so it reads as a grey box |
| Every card in a grid | Noisy, low contrast and slow |
| Glass inside glass | Double blur turns to mud |

Check text contrast where it is worst: raise `--glass-tint` until body text passes 4.5:1 over every part of the background it can scroll across.
