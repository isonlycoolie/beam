# Code Of Conduct

Beam follows a simple standard: be respectful, specific, and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss technical disagreements with evidence.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused.
- Respect privacy and do not share secrets or private design data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Publishing credentials, private files, or sensitive customer data.
- Spam or unrelated promotion.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep the project usable and safe.
